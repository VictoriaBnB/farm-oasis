# Asking guests for a Google review

Email, text and the QR card point guests at **victoriabnb.ca/review** (the page forwards to the Google review form).
Direct review link, if you ever need it raw:
`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJV_3pLQhpj1QRwMuoU19c6kI`

**Airbnb messages are the exception.** Airbnb's filter blocks anything that looks like a link, email or phone number
("Links and contact info can't be shared until after a new reservation is confirmed") — even `victoriabnb.ca/review`
with no `https://` — so the Airbnb version below contains no web address at all. Don't try to disguise it
("victoriabnb dot ca"); Airbnb flags that too and it reads as sneaky. The Google-search route works: searching
Google or Google Maps for "Modern Farm Oasis North Saanich" goes straight to the listing (checked 2026-10-30).

Is asking for a Google review allowed? Yes. Airbnb's policy (help article 2799, "Pricing and payment transparency",
the renamed Off-Platform Policy, read 2026-10-30) only forbids off-platform *payments and bookings*: communicating
off Airbnb before booking, links that take people off Airbnb or prompt another booking method, discounts for booking
direct, rebooking/extending off-platform, collecting mandatory fees outside Airbnb. Asking for a review elsewhere is
not on the list. The automated filter (article 209) only pattern-matches "contact information or references to other
sites, including external links". So: no web address, no phone/email, nothing about booking direct or saving on
fees, and avoid the word "listing" — say "our place on Google" / "Google Maps" instead.

Rules of thumb: ask everyone (no cherry-picking), never offer anything in return (Google removes incentivised
reviews and can suspend the profile), one ask per guest, keep it short.

## 1. Airbnb message to past guests (no link, no address — passes Airbnb's filter)

The one Jessica sends (mention their stay and thank them for their Airbnb review — it makes clear nobody is being steered away from Airbnb):

> Jessica from the Farm Oasis here. Thanks again for staying with us back in [March] and the great review! We've just set up our place on Google and have no reviews on it yet. If you have a minute, would you search Google for "Modern Farm Oasis North Saanich" and tap "Write a review"? A line or two is plenty. No pressure, and thank you either way. — Jessica & Ryan

Longer version:

> Hi [Name],
>
> Jessica here from the Farm Oasis in North Saanich — thank you again for staying with us [in July]. It was a pleasure having you.
>
> A small favour, if you have a minute: we've just put the farm on Google Maps and, as a little farm stay, we don't have a single review there yet. Would you mind sharing a line or two about your stay? Just search Google or Google Maps for "Modern Farm Oasis North Saanich" — we come straight up — and tap "Write a review".
>
> No pressure at all, and thank you either way. We'd love to welcome you back.
>
> Jessica & Ryan

## 2. Email / text after checkout (direct-booking guests)

> Subject: Thank you for staying at the Farm Oasis
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for staying with us — we hope the farm, the beach and the quiet sent you home rested.
>
> If you enjoyed your stay, would you leave us a short Google review? It takes about a minute and genuinely helps other travellers find us: https://victoriabnb.ca/review
>
> And if anything wasn't right, please tell us directly so we can fix it for the next guest.
>
> Warmly,
> Jessica & Ryan
> New Bright Modern Farm Oasis · North Saanich, BC

## 3. Short version (SMS)

> Thanks again for staying at the Farm Oasis! If you have a minute, a quick Google review would mean a lot: victoriabnb.ca/review — Jessica & Ryan
