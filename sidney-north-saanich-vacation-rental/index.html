Explore the peninsula

Sidney-by-the-Sea, Five Minutes Away

Sidney is a walkable seaside town with a waterfront walkway, a fishing pier, cafés, restaurants and independent bookshops along Beacon Avenue, and the Shaw Centre for the Salish Sea aquarium. Boat and whale-watching tours leave from the waterfront, with the Gulf Islands on the horizon.

North Saanich wraps around it: farmland, forest trails at John Dean Provincial Park, and Pat Bay Beach, which is a two-minute walk from our door. Butchart Gardens is 15 minutes away in Brentwood Bay.

After a day out, come back to space of your own — a private entrance, a full kitchen, in-suite laundry and a patio overlooking the farm.

Looking for a bed & breakfast?

A Private Alternative to a B&B

Many guests find us while searching for a bed and breakfast in Saanich or Sidney. Our rentals are self-catering — breakfast isn't served — but you get what a B&B room can't offer: separate bedrooms with their own bathrooms, a full kitchen and a living room to yourselves.

Each stay is stocked with cooking basics, coffee and a coffee maker, and Sidney's grocery stores and cafés are 5 minutes away for breakfast out.

Planning a longer stay? Weekly and monthly discounts are listed on our rates page.
