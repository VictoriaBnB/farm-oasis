The Suite: Two Bedrooms, a Loft and the Farm Out the Window

A brand-new, self-contained suite on our North Saanich farm: two bedrooms with their own full bathrooms, a queen loft, a bright open kitchen and a patio looking over the fields. Five minutes from Sidney, three from the airport.

Everything New, Nothing Shared

The suite is the newer of our two rentals, finished in 2025 with new appliances, furniture and fixtures throughout. It has its own entrance, so you come and go without passing through anyone else's space, and the patio outside the door has seating for morning coffee or an evening glass of something from Sea Cider down the road.

Inside, the living, dining and kitchen area is one bright open room with high ceilings and big windows onto the farm. The kitchen is fully equipped — full-size fridge and freezer, oven and stovetop, dishwasher, microwave, blender, kettle, toaster, and the pots, pans, dishes and basics you need to cook properly. A washer and dryer are in the suite.

Both bedrooms have a full ensuite bathroom. The master has a king bed and a large bathroom with a walk-in rain shower; the second bedroom has a queen bed and its own full bath. Up the stairs, the loft holds a third queen bed, and a powder room off the living area serves the loft and guests.

Outside the door: eagles over the fields, deer at the fence line, herons on the shore. Pat Bay Beach is a two-minute walk.

North Saanich, BC

Where You Are

The farm sits on the Saanich Peninsula between Pat Bay and the airport, which means almost everything is close: the beach is a short walk, Sidney's waterfront and shops are five minutes, the Swartz Bay ferry ten, Butchart Gardens fifteen and downtown Victoria about thirty-five.

If you're flying in late or out early, this is as convenient as it gets without sleeping in the terminal.

Good to know

Suite Questions

Who is the suite best for?

Couples, small families and two couples travelling together. There are two bedrooms with their own full bathrooms, a queen bed in the loft and a powder room off the living area, so up to six people can stay without anyone sharing a bathroom with strangers.

Is the suite private?

Yes. It has its own entrance and patio, its own kitchen and laundry, and it is one of only two rentals on the farm. The second rental, the Smart Home, is a separate house.

What are the beds?

A king bed in the master bedroom, a queen in the second bedroom and a queen in the loft. Premium linens, extra pillows and blankets are provided.

How do I check in?

Self check-in from 3:00 PM through the private entrance. Check-out is 10:00 AM. We send arrival details before your stay.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking on the property.

Can we rent a car nearby?

The airport is 3 minutes away with the usual rental desks. If you'd rather book a car through Turo, ask us and we'll share a link.

Are pets allowed?

No. The suite is pet-free and strictly smoke- and vape-free.
