First night or last night

Easier Than an Airport Hotel

Victoria International Airport (YYJ) is in North Saanich, three minutes' drive from our property. That makes the farm an easy first or last night on the island: land late and be in bed soon after, or drop off your rental car and catch an early flight without a long drive in from Victoria.

Unlike an airport hotel, you get a whole space — separate bedrooms with their own bathrooms, a full kitchen, a washer and dryer, and free parking at the door — plus farm views and a beach two minutes' walk away.

Plane-spotters take note: one guest listed watching the airplanes among the highlights of her stay.

Start Your Island Trip Here

The airport has rental car counters, and the farm is your first stop on the way to everything else: Sidney in 5 minutes, the Swartz Bay ferry in 10, Butchart Gardens in 15 and downtown Victoria in about 35.

Catching the ferry to the mainland or the Gulf Islands after your stay? Swartz Bay is just up the road.

Good to know

Questions

How far is it from Victoria Airport?

About a 3-minute drive.

Can we check in late after an evening flight?

Yes. Check-in is from 3 PM with self check-in and a private entrance — just tell us your flight time in your inquiry.

Is there parking for a rental car?

Yes, free parking on the premises.

Is breakfast included?

No — we're a self-catering stay rather than a traditional B&B. Both rentals have a full kitchen with cooking basics, coffee and a coffee maker, and Sidney's grocery stores and cafés are about 5 minutes away.
