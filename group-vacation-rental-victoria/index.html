Built for groups

A 4-Bedroom Home Where Everyone Gets Their Own Bathroom

The Smart Home has four bedrooms — one king and three queens — and every one has its own private bathroom, with 5.5 bathrooms in total. Nobody waits for the shower.

The open-concept living area and chef's kitchen are made for being together: the kitchen and dining room seat 12, and the patio shares the same sweeping farm views as the rest of the property. There's a home gym, a home office, 1.5 gigabit WiFi and a Samsung Frame TV with Sonos sound.

The home is solar-powered and wheelchair accessible. Travelling with more than 8? The 2-bedroom + loft Suite on the same property sleeps another 6 — send us an inquiry about booking both.

Questions

How many guests does the Smart Home sleep?

Up to 8 guests, in four bedrooms (one king and three queens), each with a private bathroom.
