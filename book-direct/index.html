How a Direct Booking Works

1

Send your dates

Use the inquiry form: pick the Suite or the Smart Home, your check-in and check-out, and how many guests. The form shows a price estimate instantly, discounts included.

What you pay

Where the Saving Comes From

Platforms charge guests a service fee on top of the host's price — typically around 14% of the subtotal — and charge the host another fee on their side. When you book direct, neither applies, and we pass that on: our direct nightly rates are set below our Airbnb pricing to begin with.

Longer stays cost less per night — , and a flat monthly rate for the longest stays. Prices are in Canadian dollars; your written quote is the number you'll pay.

Good to know

Direct Booking Questions

Is it safe to book direct?

You're booking with the same hosts and the same two homes you'll find on Airbnb, where we're a Superhost with more than 80 five-star reviews. The Suite is registered with the Province of BC (registration H295535660). We confirm everything in writing before any deposit is paid.

Is it really cheaper than Airbnb?

Yes. Our direct rates are set below our Airbnb pricing, there's no platform guest fee on top, and longer stays are discounted further than anywhere else we list.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer from a Canadian bank account: a 50% deposit holds your dates and we confirm the balance due date in your quote. Travelling from the US or overseas? Say so in your inquiry and we'll let you know your options.

What does the quote include?

The nightly rate for your dates, any long-stay discount, and the one-time cleaning fee. We'll spell out the total in writing so there are no surprises.

What is the cancellation policy?

Moderate: a full refund if you cancel at least 5 days before check-in, and a partial refund inside 5 days.

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within a few hours during the day, and always within 24 hours.

Can I check availability first?

Yes. The calendar on the home page syncs from our booking calendars every few hours. If your dates show as free, send the inquiry and we'll confirm them.
