Who stays a month

Somewhere to Land for a While

Monthly guests come to us for all the ordinary reasons a month somewhere takes: relocating to the Saanich Peninsula or Victoria and house-hunting from a comfortable base; a renovation or insurance claim that has put the family out of the house; visiting professionals and crews working at the airport, the marine-science and aviation employers around it, or the hospital in Saanichton; family visiting for a new baby or a long recovery; and winter escapes from colder parts of the country.

What you get is a home rather than a hotel room. The Suite has two bedrooms with their own bathrooms plus a loft, a full kitchen, in-suite laundry and a patio. The Smart Home has four bedrooms with four private bathrooms, a kitchen that seats twelve, a home office, a gym and gigabit fibre — enough room for a family or a crew to work and live without getting in each other's way.

Both are self check-in, with parking at the door, and you are on a quiet farm road with a beach at the end of it. Sidney's grocery stores, pharmacies, cafés and the waterfront are five minutes away.

Good to know

Monthly Stay Questions

What is the minimum stay for the monthly rate?

30 nights for the Suite and 28 nights for the Smart Home. The weekly rate applies from 7 nights.

What's included in a monthly stay?

Everything a nightly guest gets: furniture, linens and towels, a fully equipped kitchen, washer and dryer, high-speed internet, utilities, parking and the patio. The Smart Home also has a dedicated home office and gigabit fibre.

Is there a cleaning fee on long stays?

One cleaning fee per stay ($150 for the Suite, $175 for the Smart Home). If you'd like mid-stay cleaning, ask and we'll quote it.

Can I stay longer than a month?

Usually, yes. Tell us your dates in the inquiry and we'll confirm availability and a quote for the full period.

Is it close to the hospital and the airport?

Saanich Peninsula Hospital in Saanichton is about 10 minutes, Victoria Airport is 3 minutes, and Victoria General Hospital is roughly 25 minutes.

Are pets allowed on long stays?

No. Both rentals are pet-free and smoke- and vape-free for every length of stay.
